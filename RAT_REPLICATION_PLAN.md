Scope: the real-rat panels of Schneider et al., Nature 2023, Figures 1c-e and 2b-d. Figure 1b's latent reconstruction R² uses synthetic ground truth and is **not** a rat metric. Figure 2d reconstructs/decodes rat position from the embedding on test data: report position median absolute error in cm and R², not latent recovery.

Data: the four official 25-ms CA1 recordings (achilles, buddy, cicero, gatsby), copied byte-for-byte from the existing local download into `data/rat_hippocampus/`. No NeuroBridge model, split, preprocessing, checkpoint, or metric result is imported.

First stage (paper-style descriptive Figure 1): reproduce the official CEBRA consistency demo with offset10-model, 3D cosine output, batch 512, learning rate 3e-4, offset 10, and 8000 updates; Time temperature 1.12, Behavior temperature 1.0. Train one independently seeded model per rat and mode. Save checkpoints, frozen embeddings, provenance, 3D plots, and cross-rat consistency matrices. Seed 42 is the first qualification/replication run; additional seeds must be stored separately, without overwriting it. The paper's Figure 1e reports a mean over 10 runs, so a one-seed matrix must not be presented as an exact replication of that panel.

Second stage (Figure 2, transferable benchmark): train separate rat models with position-only, direction-only, and position+direction supervision, plus label-shuffled controls. Apply the *same* downstream decoder to every frozen embedding, regardless of which labels were supplied during representation training. A CEBRA-Time model provides the label-free contrast. The official Figure 2 demo uses a contiguous 80/20 split and a 36-neighbor cosine kNN decoder; our primary thesis benchmark instead uses the official rat dataset's 3-fold complete-trial train/validation/test partition to avoid declaring overlapping or adjacent windows independent. Preserve the paper-style 80/20 result as a separately labelled reproduction, not as the primary generalization claim. Fit encoders and decoders on train only; use validation for any selection; test remains untouched until the protocol is fixed.

Primary transferable outcomes: held-out position median absolute error (cm), position R², and direction balanced accuracy; present each separately. Secondary: across-run and cross-rat consistency R² on behavior-aligned embeddings, 3D geometry, and computational cost. Show 3D interactive HTML and static plots from frozen embeddings; plots are not numerical evidence by themselves. A paper-style time-bin label shuffle and a stricter trial-structured null must be labelled as different null hypotheses; never treat one as the other.

Third stage (completed 12 Oct 2026): PCA/UMAP/openTSNE baseline campaign is complete (252/252; PCA 12, UMAP 120, openTSNE 120). Reducers are fit on training windows and valid/test are transformed; the scoring support excludes trial-boundary windows. Frozen embeddings and decoding are in `results/rat_figure_gap_closure_v1/figure2d_metrics/`; the older partial checkpoint `results/rat_manifold_decoding_v2_umap_complete/` is historical and superseded for method coverage. Baselines are 2D while CEBRA embeddings are 32D, so decoding is not dimension-matched. Figure panels were rendered downstream in the versioned figure branch. Conv-piVAE is a separate 120-fit campaign and is now complete; it was not part of the PCA/UMAP/openTSNE baseline grid. autoLFADS remains excluded.

The user's future soft-target loss/model must run on the identical source data, trial split, labels, decoder, and metrics; its supervised condition must be declared, and it must not be compared as if it were label-free. No synthetic latent reconstruction score will be relabeled as a rat reconstruction score.
